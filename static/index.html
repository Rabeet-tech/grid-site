Three teams

01

Research and Development

Takes on open problems across every department. The team reads the literature, runs experiments and builds working prototypes, then writes up what it finds for competitions, conferences and journals.

  • Research papers and reviews
  • Prototypes and experiments
  • Robotics and embedded systems
Meet the team
02

Software

Designs, builds and maintains the software G.R.I.D ships, from web and mobile apps to the tools behind research projects. It also forms the society's hackathon teams and runs code reviews.

  • Web and mobile apps
  • AI and data tools
  • Hackathons and open source
Meet the team
03

Marketing and Comms

Tells people what G.R.I.D is building. The team runs social media, designs posters and event material, writes up project launches and handles outreach to sponsors and other societies.

  • Social media and content
  • Design and branding
  • Events, sponsors and outreach
